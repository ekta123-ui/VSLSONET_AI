RESEARCH NOTES

Methodology & limitations

VALOSENT AI separates sentiment research from professional match analytics.

SENTIMENT DATASET

VALORANT Metacritic Reviews

1,009 player reviews from a June 2020 snapshot. Labels are derived from scores: 0-4 Negative, 5-6 Neutral, 7-10 Positive. They are not human-annotated sentiment labels.

License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The source citation and restrictions are recorded with the review data.

MATCH DATASET

Professional match snapshot

24,924 validated map groups from April 2021 through April 2023, with map scores, agent picks, and player metrics. Match, event, and region identifiers are absent.

The source is a historical VLR.gg scrape distributed on Kaggle under CC0. T/CT fields are player metrics, not side win rates.

RECOMMENDATION METHOD

Observed data, explicit support

Overall map recommendations rank the 95% Wilson lower bound of observed map win rate after a minimum-pick threshold. Attack/defense views rank median T/CT ACS and label it as ACS, not win rate.

Historical composition and pairing views require repeated observations. Low-support combinations are hidden as insufficient data.

LIMITATIONS

Interpret carefully

The match data ends in April 2023 and contains nine maps. Some metric cells are missing or impossible and are set to unavailable. Team names and rows have no event/region context.

Topics and patch-level sentiment are not implemented because the review data has no topic or patch annotations.
